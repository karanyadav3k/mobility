Privacy Policy & User Terms (गोपनीयता नीति)

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 | App: GatiConnect (Universal Mobility & Capacity Network)

1. Information We Collect (हम क्या जानकारी एकत्र करते हैं)

  • Phone Number & OTP: Used solely for secure account authentication and instant session recovery.
  • Profile Information (Optional): Full Name, Email, Emergency Contact, Driving License (DL), and Vehicle Details. These are used to calculate the transparent Trust Score (70-100) to keep the community safe.
  • Route & Trip Listings: Source, Destination, Preferred timing, and vehicle capacity.

2. How We Use Your Data (डेटा का उपयोग)

  • Smart Matchmaking: To connect empty seats, commuters, and parcels traveling on the exact same route.
  • Escrow Security: Payments are held safely in escrow and only released upon successful delivery verification via two-factor OTP.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell or share your personal data with advertisers or third-party marketing agencies.

3. Location & Device Permissions

Location data is used solely to find nearby carpools, parcels, and transport routes in real-time. Background location is only accessed when actively sharing a trip.

4. Data Deletion & Account Closure (डेटा हटाना)

Users have full control over their data. You can delete your listings or request complete account deletion at any time by contacting support at support@gaticonnect.in.
